Privacy & medical disclaimer

Medical disclaimer

The information on this website — including test descriptions and preparation advice — is general and educational. It is not a diagnosis, medical advice or a substitute for consultation with a qualified doctor. Laboratory results must be interpreted by a doctor together with your symptoms, examination and history. Always follow the instructions of your treating doctor. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call 108.

What we collect

  • Booking requests: patient name, mobile number, age, sex, requested tests, preferred date and time, referring doctor, optional address and notes.
  • Messages and doctor enquiries: the name, contact details and message you send.
  • Your test list is stored only on your own device (browser storage) until you send or clear it.
  • WhatsApp: when you tap “Send on WhatsApp”, the message is sent through WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

How we use it

We use these details only to contact you about your request, confirm your visit and preparation, and answer your query. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Form submissions are stored securely by our website hosting provider and are accessible only to authorised laboratory staff.
