Questions

Before you visit

Do I need to fast before my test?

Only some tests need fasting — mainly fasting blood sugar, the glucose tolerance test and, if your doctor asks, the lipid profile. Water is allowed. Each test in our directory shows its preparation. See the preparation guide.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Please bring your prescription if you have one. It helps us run exactly what your doctor needs and lets your doctor interpret the result properly.

Should I take my regular medicines?

Usually yes, but some tests are affected by medicines (for example blood thinners, thyroxine, steroids, iron or biotin supplements). Ask your doctor and tell the lab what you take.

What if my test is not listed?

Call or WhatsApp us. Many specialised tests are sent to our partner reference laboratories.

What should I do in an emergency?

Chest pain, breathlessness, fainting, heavy bleeding or confusion need urgent care. Go to the nearest hospital or call 108 — do not wait for a lab test.
