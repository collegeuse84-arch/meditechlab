Get the Meditech app

The Meditech app installs straight from this website — no app store, no sign-up, and it uses very little storage. It opens full-screen from your home screen, and the test directory and preparation guide keep working with a weak or no connection.

{{icon:phoneapp}}

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open Meditech Lab from your home screen.
{{icon:phoneapp}}

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open Meditech Lab from your home screen.
{{icon:home}}

Computer (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Click Install.
  3. Meditech opens in its own window.
{{icon:shield}}

What the app can do

  • Search all tests with sample and preparation
  • Save your test list and send it on WhatsApp
  • Send booking requests
  • Call the lab in one tap
