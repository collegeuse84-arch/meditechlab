Specimen quick reference

Common sample requirements

SpecimenTypical tests
EDTA whole bloodCBC, ESR*, HbA1c, blood group, retic, G6PD, Hb electrophoresis, HLA B27, malaria
Sodium citratePT-INR, APTT (correct fill volume essential)
FluorideGlucose — fasting, PP, random, GTT
SerumLFT, RFT, lipids, thyroid, hormones, vitamins, serology, tumour markers, cardiac markers
Sterile containerUrine, stool, semen and culture specimens
10% neutral buffered formalinHistopathology (about 10× tissue volume)

*ESR on EDTA or citrated blood as per lab method. Please call the lab to confirm requirements for any specialised or send-out test.

Helpful details on the requisition

  • Patient name, age, sex and mobile number
  • Clinical details or provisional diagnosis
  • Relevant medicines — anticoagulants, thyroxine, steroids, antibiotics
  • Gestational age and scan details for antenatal screening
  • Collection time for timed samples (PP, GTT, cortisol)
